TMS Page — Layout Options

Three ways to arrange the 5 signals. Pick the one that feels most natural to scan.

A

Hero Trend — OTIF front and center

Exception banner full-width at top. OTIF trend chart gets hero treatment (full width). Carrier ranking + POD aging in a 2-col row below. Volume bars at the bottom.

⚠ Exception Banner (full width)
📈 OTIF Trend — 8 weeks (full width, tall chart)
🏆 Carrier OTIF Ranking
⏱ POD Aging Buckets
📊 Daily Volume (this week vs last week)
Best for: OTIF is the KPI you care about most. Trend story is front and center.
B

Exception First — action items at the top

Exception banner + 3 KPI tiles at the top (active count, OTIF %, pending PODs). Then 2×2 grid: OTIF trend / Carrier ranking / Volume / POD aging. Data-dense but scannable.

⚠ Exception Banner + 3 KPI counters (1 row)
📈 OTIF Trend 8w
🏆 Carrier Ranking
📊 Daily Volume
⏱ POD Aging
Best for: mornings — you want to know immediately if anything is broken, then scan all metrics equally.
C

Split Panel — status left, trends right

Left column (1/3): live status — exception banner, KPI tiles, POD aging. Right column (2/3): trend charts — OTIF sparkline (tall) and volume bars stacked. Carrier ranking below full-width.

⚠ Exceptions
Active: 247
OTIF: 99%
POD 1-3일: 12
POD 4-7일: 3
POD 7일+: 0
📈 OTIF 8w trend (tall)

📊 Volume this/last week
🏆 Carrier OTIF Ranking (full width table)
Best for: glancing left for status, right for trend story. Clear visual separation of "now" vs "over time".
